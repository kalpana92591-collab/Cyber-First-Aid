🛡️ Two-Factor Authentication Guide

Add another layer of protection to your accounts.

  1. Open your account's official security settings.
  2. Find Two-Factor Authentication or Two-Step Verification.
  3. Choose an authenticator app or security key when available.
  4. Save backup codes somewhere secure.
  5. Never share verification codes with anyone.

Prefer phishing-resistant passkeys or security keys when supported.
